# Medatrax reference

Single source of truth for the Medatrax NP portal. [clinical-note](../skills/clinical-note/SKILL.md) emits entry fields in the order below so they can be tabbed straight into the form.

Portal: `np.medatrax.com` — **not** `medatrax.com`, which is the Anesthesia-defaulted marketing login.

Read from the live portal. Picklist strings below are exact — match them character for character, including `Wyoming County Health Dept.` with its period and `New River Health - Oak Hill` with spaced hyphen.

## Two clocks, and only one counts

Patient encounters and clinical hours are **separate systems**:

- **Patient encounters** (`patient.aspx`) log a `Visit Time` that is `0:15` on every entry. At fifteen minutes each, 360 hours would take 1,440 encounters — these do not accrue the requirement.
- **Time Log** (`timesheetentry.aspx`) is where total hours at the clinical site are entered, and is almost certainly what the 360 hours are counted from.

Log encounters for the case requirement; log hours in the Time Log. Missing either one leaves a hole the other cannot fill.

## Forms

Selected from `forms/`. The two that matter here map exactly onto the skill's branches:

| Medatrax form | Branch |
| --- | --- |
| `1. FNP: H & P` | [HP.md](../skills/clinical-note/HP.md) |
| `2. FNP: Comprehensive Soap Note` | [SOAP.md](../skills/clinical-note/SOAP.md) |

The remaining forms are PMHNP — Group Note (v1), Note (v5), Short Note (v2), Therapy Note (v2) — plus screening tools: Anxiety/GAD, CAGE Aid, CMHA, Depression/PHQ-9, MOCA, PCAM. No branch covers these yet.

**`2. FNP: Comprehensive Soap Note` has six sections**, not four: Subjective Data, Objective Data, Assessment/Analysis, Plan, **Intervention**, **Evaluation**.

Forms carry three status flags, filterable: Administrator Feedback, Completed, Graded.

### Working rule

> The system auto saves forms every 2 minutes. Only have 1 form/window open at a time, as the auto save could override progress.

Never leave a form open in a second tab. Draft the note outside Medatrax, paste once, close.

## Per-encounter fields

| # | Field | Type | Example | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Patient Reference | auto | `92F72BF3B55C48D` | **generated by Medatrax** — never supply a name |
| 2 | Visit Date | date | 4/8/2026 | given |
| 3 | Interaction Level | picklist | Level 5 | given — option range unconfirmed |
| 4 | Race/Ethnicity | picklist | Caucasian/White, African American/Black | given — full list unconfirmed |
| 5 | Gender | picklist | Female | given |
| 6 | Age | number + unit | 35 Years | given |
| 7 | Marital Status at First Contact | picklist | Married | given |
| 8 | Primary Payment Method | picklist | Commercial Insurance | given |
| 9 | Start Time / End Time | time | 16:45 / 17:00 | given |
| 10 | Visit Time | duration | 0:15 | derived from start and end |
| 11 | Location | picklist | New River Health - Oak Hill | given |
| 12 | Preceptor | picklist | Lindley,Juddson | from the day file |
| 13 | Patient Height | number (in) | 64 in | given |
| 14 | BMI | number | 28.7 | derived from height and weight |
| 15 | Case Type | picklist | Endocrine Disorder | given |
| 16 | Blood Pressure | text | 122/78 mmHg | given |
| 17 | Respiratory Rate | number | 16/min | given |
| 18 | Patient Time | picklist | Womens Health | given |
| 19 | Reviewed | flag | — | set by the preceptor |

Medatrax carries BP, RR, Height and BMI but **not HR, temperature, SpO2, or weight**. The note rubric wants a complete vital set, so those four are routinely filled and listed for confirmation. Any vital Medatrax does carry is a given the note must match exactly.

## Add Visit Data

Attached per visit from a dropdown on the patient detail page:

```
ICD-10-CM        Medications        FNP Clinical Experience Check List
FNP EMT/ICD Codes    Procedure Codes    Drug List
```

Plus PMHNP-only categories: E & M Codes, PMH Procedures/Skills, PMHNP Therapies, PMHNP Type of Interaction Level.

`ICD-10-CM`, `Procedure Codes`, and `FNP EMT/ICD Codes` are where [icd10-cpt](../skills/icd10-cpt/SKILL.md) output lands.
