## Conflicts

**CONFLICT: antiviral-response** - `plateau: HBV DNA decline <=1 log/mL in past 6 mo; breakthrough: increase >=1 log or >100 IU/mL after undetectability; slow decline: quantifiable after 96 wk and <5-log decline` differs from `after changing antivirals, HBV DNA every 3 mo until undetectable, then every 6 mo`.
Suboptimal response is divided into different
measurements: plateau is `HBV DNA decline <=1 log/mL in 6 mo`; breakthrough is an
increase `>=1 log` or `>100 IU/mL after undetectability`; slow decline is quantifiable
HBV DNA after `96 wk` with `<5-log decline`; and post-change monitoring is every `3 mo`
until undetectable, then every `6 mo`.

**CONFLICT: chb-phase** - `HBeAg-negative immune active: ALT >=2 x ULN and HBV DNA >=2000 IU/mL; inactive: normal ALT and HBV DNA <2000 IU/mL` differs from `functional cure: HBsAg negative and HBV DNA undetectable off treatment >=6 mo; partial cure: HBsAg <100 IU/mL and HBV DNA undetectable off treatment >=6 mo`.
The same HBsAg-positive population is partitioned by HBeAg,
ALT, and HBV DNA. Immune tolerant uses `HBV DNA >=10,000,000 IU/mL` with ALT `<25 U/L`
in women or `<35 U/L` in men. HBeAg-positive immune active uses `ALT >=2 x ULN` and
`HBV DNA >=20,000 IU/mL`; its indeterminate bands use `HBV DNA >10,000,000 IU/mL`
with ALT `1 to <2 x ULN`, or `HBV DNA >=20,000 to <=10,000,000 IU/mL` with ALT `<ULN`
or `1 to <2 x ULN`. HBeAg-negative immune active uses `ALT >=2 x ULN` and `HBV DNA
>=2,000 IU/mL`; inactive disease uses normal ALT with `HBV DNA <2,000 IU/mL`.
Functional cure requires HBsAg negative and undetectable HBV DNA off treatment for

**CONFLICT: hcc-surveillance-age** - Surveillance thresholds differ by population:

`>40 y` and women `>50 y`. Table 4 separately supports surveillance as early as the
`third decade` for persons from Africa and at `PAGE-B >=10` for antiviral-treated persons.

**CONFLICT: hcc-surveillance-interval** - The values `approximately every 6 mo` and
`every 6 mo` express the same semiannual surveillance interval in narrative guidance
and the cirrhosis algorithm.

**CONFLICT: immune-active-treatment** - HBeAg-positive disease uses `HBV DNA >=20,000
IU/mL` with ALT `>=2 x ULN`, whereas HBeAg-negative disease uses `HBV DNA >=2,000
IU/mL` with ALT `>=2 x ULN`.

**CONFLICT: immune-tolerant-treatment** - `treat if age >40 y, inflammation grade >=2, or fibrosis stage >=F2; under age 40 use shared decision-making` differs from `VCTE >=8 kPa identifies F2 or higher fibrosis for treatment consideration` and `consider treatment if age >=40 y or fibrosis >=F2`.
Treatment eligibility is expressed as age
`>40 y` in Recommendation 3 but `>=40 y` in Figure 2, inflammation grade `>=2`,
fibrosis `>=F2`, or VCTE `>=8 kPa`; under age `40 y` the recommendation calls for
shared decision-making. These are alternative qualifying features, not interchangeable
measurements.

**CONFLICT: indeterminate-treatment** - `age >40 y, male sex, or platelets <180 k/mm3 may favor treatment; F3/F4 should be treated` differs from `FIB-4 >1.45 or VCTE >=8 kPa indicates high sensitivity for advanced fibrosis`.

also used fibrosis `>=F2`, inflammation `>=A3/grade 3`, or persistently elevated ALT
`>ULN` with HBV DNA `>=2,000 IU/mL`, especially after age `40 y`.

**CONFLICT: infant-prophylaxis** - `HBIG and birth-dose HBV vaccination within 24 h of birth` differs from `HBIG and HBV vaccine within 12 h of birth; complete vaccine series; test HBsAg and anti-HBs at age 9-12 mo` and `administer HBIG within 12 h and HBV vaccine within 24 h of birth`.

within `24 h`; Figure 1 on page 7 places both HBIG and vaccine within `12 h`; Figure 4

**CONFLICT: na-restart** - `restart immediately for HBV DNA >=10,000 IU/mL regardless of ALT, ALT >=5 x ULN regardless of HBV DNA, bilirubin >2.5 mg/dL, or hepatic decompensation` differs from `may restart when HBV DNA >=2000 IU/mL and ALT >=2 x ULN`, `restart immediately for HBV DNA >=10,000 IU/mL regardless of ALT, ALT >5 x ULN regardless of HBV DNA, bilirubin >2.5 mg/dL, or hepatic decompensation`, and `may restart when HBV DNA >=2000 IU/mL and ALT >2 x ULN`.

**CONFLICT: na-suppression-duration** - `at least 3 y of NA therapy with undetectable HBV DNA for at least 2 y before considering withdrawal` differs from `consider withdrawal after HBV DNA suppression for >=3 y, with no cirrhosis and HBsAg <100 IU/mL`.

**CONFLICT: pregnancy-antiviral** - `HBV DNA >200,000 IU/mL: start TDF or TAF at gestational week 28` differs from `if presenting for perinatal care after gestational week 28, start TDF or TAF immediately`.
