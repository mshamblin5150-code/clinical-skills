## Conflicts

**CONFLICT: adult-major-scd-risk-thresholds** — These are alternative limbs of one 2024 ICD recommendation, not competing cutoff estimates: `>=1 major risk factor; offer ICD`; `>=1 HCM-related death in a first-degree or close relative age <=50 years`; `LV wall >=30 mm`; `>=1 recent episode of suspected arrhythmic syncope`; `LV apical aneurysm with transmural scar or LGE`; and `EF <50%`. The original combined display was `family HCM death age <=50; LV wall >=30 mm; >=1 recent arrhythmic syncope; apical aneurysm with scar/LGE; EF <50%`.

**CONFLICT: variant-reevaluation-interval** — The 2024 top-level summary states `every 2-3 years`, while the detailed genetics discussion says `every few years`; both call for periodic reassessment of variant classification, and the sheet preserves the difference in precision.

**CONFLICT: exercise-tte-gradient-threshold** — The symptomatic recommendation says `no resting or provocable gradient >=50 mm Hg; perform exercise TTE when symptomatic`, while the asymptomatic recommendation says `no resting or provocable gradient >=50 mm Hg; exercise TTE reasonable when asymptomatic`; the gradient boundary agrees and the recommendation strength differs by symptoms.

**CONFLICT: systolic-dysfunction-ef-threshold** — The same systolic-dysfunction boundary carries three separate management actions: `LVEF <50%; discontinue myosin inhibitor`; `LVEF <50%; negative inotrope discontinuation reasonable`; and `persistent LVEF <50%; ICD can be beneficial`.

**CONFLICT: vigorous-activity-review-interval** — The two shared-decision settings use the same cadence but preserve their exact distinct wording: `annual and repeated annually`; and, independently, `annual`.

**CONFLICT: pediatric diagnostic z-score** — Both full guidelines describe the
traditional pediatric threshold as body-size-adjusted z-score >=2, then distinguish
pretest probability. The 2024 current text states z-score >2.5 for an asymptomatic
child without family history and >2 for a child with a definitive family history or
positive genetic test. The executive summary omits this narrative definition. The
sheet records the 2024 stratified thresholds rather than collapsing them to the
traditional >=2 convention.

**CONFLICT: adult ICD apical-aneurysm risk marker** — The 2020 recommendation treats
an LV apical aneurysm as a major risk factor independent of size. The 2024
recommendation narrows that wording to an LV apical aneurysm with transmural scar or
LGE. This is a material change; the current 2024 wording governs the row.

**CONFLICT: post-2020 therapy** — The 2020 obstructive-HCM escalation recommendation
offers disopyramide or SRT after beta blocker or nondihydropyridine calcium-channel
blocker failure. The 2024 recommendation adds a cardiac myosin inhibitor for adults
and separately requires stopping a myosin inhibitor when LVEF remains below 50%.
The 2020 documents cannot be read as contradicting a therapy they predate.

**CONFLICT: source overlap** — The 2020 executive summary repeats recommendation
tables from the 2020 full guideline with different PDF page numbers. It is evidence
of the same 2020 rule, not a second source in favor of that rule. The 2024 guideline
repeats many unchanged thresholds and updates others; current rows cite the 2024
source, while the explicitly historical exercise-BP and warfarin-teratogenicity rows
retain their 2020 provenance. Coverage below accounts for all three exact
recommendation records.
