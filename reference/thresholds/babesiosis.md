## Conflicts

CONFLICT: exchange-transfusion-indication | severe-babesiosis-exchange-candidate | `consider red-cell exchange for parasitemia >10% or severe hemolytic anemia or severe pulmonary, renal, or hepatic compromise` versus `CORRECTED TABLE: consider exchange transfusion for parasitemia >10%, or for moderate-to-high parasitemia plus severe hemolytic anemia or severe pulmonary, renal, or hepatic compromise`; the formal recommendation permits exchange for >10% parasitemia alone or for severe anemia/organ compromise alone, whereas the corrected Table 1 footnote retains >10% alone but requires moderate-to-high parasitemia together with severe anemia or pulmonary, renal, or hepatic compromise in its second branch. Both source-stated boundaries are preserved rather than silently harmonized.

Atovaquone plus azithromycin is the preferred combination and clindamycin plus quinine
is the alternative generally, but the corrected table and page-11 implementation text
prefer or reserve clindamycin plus quinine when parasitemia and symptoms fail to abate
after atovaquone plus azithromycin; this is a nonresponse escalation, not a competing
initial-regimen value. The erratum's revised clindamycin and quinine intervals and
one-time intravenous-azithromycin language control the earlier presentation. The adult
severe row's azithromycin 500 mg IV every 24 hours is the usual table regimen; the
one-time 1000 mg IV dose with subsequent 500 mg daily is a narrowly qualified,
very-limited-experience option for severe immunocompromised patients.
