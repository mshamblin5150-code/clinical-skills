## Conflicts

**CONFLICT: annual-risk** - The same risk-stratification population has `low <1%`, `intermediate 1%-3%`, and `high >3%` yearly risk; these are mutually exclusive risk categories.

**CONFLICT: beta-blocker-lvef** - The source rows retain `long-term beta-blocker therapy is not recommended to improve outcomes unless MI occurred within the past year, LVEF <=50%, or another primary indication is present`; `LVEF <=40%: beta blocker recommended with or without prior MI`; `LVEF <50%: use metoprolol succinate, carvedilol, or bisoprolol and titrate to target`; `reassess indication for beta blocker >1 y after MI`; and `without previous MI or LVEF <=50%, beta blocker provides no MACE benefit absent another indication`. Their populations and intended actions differ.

**CONFLICT: bp-treatment** - The hypertension rows retain `target <130/<80 mm Hg` and `hypertension >=130 systolic and/or >=80 diastolic mm Hg: medication plus nonpharmacologic strategy`; these values distinguish the treatment threshold from the target.

**CONFLICT: dapt-duration** - Distinct post-PCI strategies are `6 months then SAPT`, `1-3 months then P2Y12 monotherapy for at least 12 months`, and, after prior MI with low bleeding risk, extension beyond `12 months` for up to `3 years`.

**CONFLICT: ldl-reduction** - The rows retain `high-intensity statin: LDL-C reduction >=50%` and `moderate-intensity statin when high intensity is contraindicated or not tolerated: LDL-C reduction 30%-49%`; statin intensity explains the distinct goals.

**CONFLICT: nonstatin-threshold** - The recurring `LDL-C >=70 mg/dL (>=1.8 mmol/L)` threshold leads to ezetimibe, PCSK9 monoclonal antibody, bempedoic acid, or inclisiran only after the row-specific risk group and prior-therapy conditions are applied; the very-high-risk PCSK9 row also permits `non-HDL-C >=100 mg/dL (>=2.6 mmol/L)`.

**CONFLICT: pci-oac-duration** - The source gives `DAPT 1-4 weeks then clopidogrel for 6 months`, aspirin plus clopidogrel up to `1 month` for high thrombotic/low bleeding risk, and possible aspirin discontinuation with DOAC alone `1 year` after PCI for low atherothrombotic risk.

**CONFLICT: sglt2-lvef** - The rows retain `LVEF <=40%: SGLT2 inhibitor recommended regardless of diabetes` and `LVEF >40%: SGLT2 inhibitor can be beneficial regardless of diabetes`; the values partition the heart-failure population.

**CONFLICT: syntax-score** - For complex multivessel CAD, `SYNTAX >33` favors CABG over PCI; for diabetes with left main disease, `SYNTAX <=33` permits PCI as an alternative to CABG. Population and anatomic complexity differ.

**CONFLICT: transplant-statin-dose** - The rows retain `atorvastatin <=10 mg daily`; `rosuvastatin <=5 mg daily`; `pravastatin <=40 mg daily`; `fluvastatin <=40 mg daily`; and `avoid simvastatin, lovastatin, and pitavastatin`. Interaction magnitude was reported with cyclosporine, and data are limited for tacrolimus, everolimus, and sirolimus.

**CONFLICT: physical-activity** - The rows retain `>=150 min/wk moderate aerobic or >=75 min/wk higher-intensity aerobic activity` and `resistance training >=2 days/wk`; the values apply to different activity modes.

**CONFLICT: saturated-fat-limit** - The page 22 rows retain `<6% of total caloric intake for patients at higher cardiovascular risk` from the prose and `<=6% of daily calories in the rendered figure` from the figure; both source presentations are preserved rather than silently normalized.
