## Conflicts

No unresolved same-population, same-quantity conflict was identified. The summary's
fall/winter influenza-and-COVID statement applies to the respiratory-season subset;
the detailed COVID-19 passage separately instructs all pregnant and lactating
individuals to receive an updated vaccine at the earliest opportunity. These nested
populations are retained rather than narrowing the detailed statement to fall or winter.

Figure 1's Tdap calendar strip says the vaccine can be administered at any time of year;
its gestational schedule box and the source-authored summary specify one dose during each
pregnancy, preferably early in weeks 27-36. Calendar season and gestational age are
different quantities and are not normalized into a contradiction.

The maternal RSV summary says eligible patients who meet criteria should receive vaccine;
Figure 1 supplies the product, 32-36-week, September-January, and local-seasonality
criteria, while Table 1 supplies the first-pregnancy and subsequent-pregnancy infant-
antibody boundary. These are complementary source-authored ACOG instructions. The cited
ACOG advisories, ACOG committee opinions, and outside studies are supporting references,
not separately imported recommendations; no action is relabeled as IDSA guidance merely
because the PDF is stored under the `IDSA/` corpus namespace.
