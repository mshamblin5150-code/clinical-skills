## Conflicts

No same-population, same-quantity internal conflict was identified. The general
preamble's direction to individualize clinical decisions does not reverse the specific
grade-D instruction to discourage screening of asymptomatic adolescent or adult males.
Symptomatic care and the unreviewed cryptorchidism population are outside that
asymptomatic recommendation rather than conflicting exceptions. The externally reported
AAFP, AAP, and ACS positions align with non-screening and remain explicitly external.
