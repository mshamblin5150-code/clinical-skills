## Conflicts

The Grade B prevention recommendation and cessation I statement apply to different
branches after tobacco-use ascertainment. Prevention applies to all youth who have not
started tobacco, regardless of risk factors. The I statement is neither for nor against
intervention for youth who already use tobacco; it preserves individualized clinical
judgment and does not make medication a recommended cessation action.

The evidence is cigarette-centered but the source applies prevention evidence to
e-cigarettes and cigars and describes the overall evidence as most applicable to
combustible products and e-cigarettes. This extrapolation is not rewritten as direct
product-specific trial evidence. No prevention modality, recipient, duration, or setting
was superior, and study contact/session counts are evidence descriptions rather than
mandatory intensity thresholds.

Source-printed AAP cessation and pharmacotherapy positions are external and more
actionable than the USPSTF cessation I statement. They remain organization-specific
rather than being merged into the USPSTF conclusion. The source-era absence of an
FDA-approved pediatric cessation medication and varenicline age-label boundary do not
erase the separately attributed AAP external position. Adult and pregnancy cessation
guidance and youth drug-use guidance address different populations or substances.
