## Conflicts

No same-source, same-population contradictory patient-action values were identified. The guideline intentionally rejects a single universal acceptance cutoff for intermediate-risk candidates: GFR 60-89 mL/min per 1.73 m2, AER 30-100 mg/d, controlled hypertension, obesity, prediabetes/type 2 diabetes, dyslipidemia, active tobacco use, prior stones, and selected prior pregnancy disorders require individualized integration against the transplant program's predetermined acceptable-risk threshold. These are not conflicts with the categorical exclusions below or categorical routine-acceptance values above.

Several values are explicitly external provenance rather than new KDIGO donor thresholds: regional general-population definitions govern normal BP, diabetes/prediabetes classification, cancer screening, recurrent-stone prevention, and routine preventive care; WHO or race-specific categories govern BMI classification; the KDIGO 2012 CKD guideline governs eGFR methods and management of donors who develop CKD; local public-health epidemiology governs additional infection testing; and local law governs ethical and legal requirements. The sheet preserves those referrals without converting them into invented universal cutoffs.
