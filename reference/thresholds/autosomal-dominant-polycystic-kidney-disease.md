CONFLICT: ultrasound-exclusion — `age 15-29 years: no cysts excludes ADPKD`, `age 30-39 years: no cysts excludes ADPKD`, and `age 40-59 years: <2 total cysts excludes ADPKD` are age-specific exclusion branches.

CONFLICT: mri-diagnosis-exclusion — `age 16-40 years: >=10 cysts diagnoses ADPKD` is the positive branch; `age 16-40 years: <5 cysts excludes ADPKD` is the negative branch.

CONFLICT: adult-bp-target — `BP >130/85 mm Hg: target <=110/75 mm Hg by HBPM if tolerated`, `BP >110/75 and <130/85 mm Hg: individualize treatment`, and `target mean SBP <120 mm Hg if tolerated` distinguish age, CKD stage, baseline BP, and measurement method.

CONFLICT: tolvaptan-initiation — `eGFR >=25 mL/min/1.73 m²: initiate tolvaptan when rapid progression is indicated`, `age >55 years: use shared individualized decision-making`, and `PROPKD score >6 supports rapid progression` are complementary eligibility and risk-classification limbs.

CONFLICT: tolvaptan-dose — `initiate 45 mg on waking and 15 mg 8 hours later` and `target 90 mg on waking and 30 mg 8 hours later` are initiation and titration-target regimens.

CONFLICT: tolvaptan-liver-monitoring — `liver-function tests monthly for first 18 months, then every 3 months` is surveillance; `ALT >3 times ULN plus bilirubin >2 times ULN meets Hy's law` is the injury threshold.

CONFLICT: liver-cyst-drainage — `consider drainage <48 hours after antibiotics for cyst >8 cm, instability, immunocompromise, sepsis, or resistant pathogen` is an early high-risk branch; `no response after 48-72 hours: evaluate further and consider drainage` is the nonresponse branch.

CONFLICT: physical-activity — the single value contains both `moderate activity >=150 minutes/week` and `strength training >=1 hour twice/week` because the practice point specifies both targets.

CONFLICT: alcohol-limit — `<=1 alcoholic drink per day` applies to females and `<=2 alcoholic drinks per day` applies to males.

CONFLICT: pediatric-abpm — `VEO-ADPKD or EO-ADPKD: annual 24-hour ABPM from age >=5 years and height >=120 cm` and `annual 24-hour ABPM when age >=5 years, height >=120 cm, and office BP >=75th percentile` are the two exact branches.
