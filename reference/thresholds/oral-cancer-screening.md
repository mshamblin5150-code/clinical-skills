## Conflicts

No unresolved same-population, same-quantity conflict was identified. The USPSTF I
statement is limited to asymptomatic adults screened by primary care providers; it does
not direct diagnostic care for symptoms or known lesions and does not govern dental or
otolaryngology screening. Risk associations do not become screening eligibility rules.

Clinical visual inspection and palpation, tissue-biopsy confirmation, and treatment are
distinct steps. The `more than 2 weeks` abnormality boundary is a reevaluation and
biopsy-consideration action, not a universal screening interval. Heterogeneous accuracy
ranges and United Kingdom dental-study results are evidence context, not primary-care
United States cutoffs.

The AAFP insufficient-evidence position, ACS age-20 periodic-exam action, and ADA
routine-exam alertness action are retained as distinct attributed external guidance.
They are not rewritten as USPSTF conclusions or merged across their different
populations and clinician settings.
