CONFLICT: hiv-vl-prophylaxis | hiv-vl-low-cd4 | `give secondary prophylaxis when CD4 <200 cells/mm3` versus `continue maintenance until CD4 consistently >200-350/mm3` | The first value is the initiation example in recommendation 65; the second is the source table's immune-reconstitution stopping range.
