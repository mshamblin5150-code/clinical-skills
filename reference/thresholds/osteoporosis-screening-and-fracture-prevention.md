**Read:** all eleven pages, including all three recommendation branches, applicability,
definitions, risk assessment, screening tests and strategies, the rendered clinician
summary and risk-tool table, intervals, treatment context, men's I-statement practice,
related USPSTF guidance, evidence review, harms, public comment, research needs,
recommendations of others, article information, disclosures, and references.

**Scoped out under ADR 0009's decision-point rule:** prevalence and burden estimates,
mobility outcomes, trial enrollment and follow-up periods, test-accuracy statistics,
fracture-effect estimates, confidence intervals, sample sizes, publication dates,
research requests, author metadata, and reference-list numbers that do not change a
screening, risk-assessment, diagnostic, counseling, treatment, or evidence boundary.

than converted into patient-care cutoffs.
