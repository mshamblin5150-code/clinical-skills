## Conflicts

The formal pregnancy recommendation uses proteinuria <1 g/day for attempting pregnancy, while later narrative describes <500 mg/day as part of a lower-risk profile. These are preserved separately: the former is the recommendation threshold and the latter is a narrower counseling profile, not a replacement threshold. Different surveillance intervals otherwise represent different posttransplant phases, pathogens, CKD stages, or treatment events. The guideline repeatedly requires individualization when rejection prevention conflicts with infection, malignancy, metabolic, reproductive, hematologic, or drug-toxicity risk.

External/source-era provenance requires special caution. Adult BP and lipid values were adapted from contemporaneous KDOQI/general-population guidance; vaccine schedules refer to contemporaneous CDC/local schedules; cancer screening refers to regional general-population guidance; diabetes criteria refer to contemporaneous ADA criteria; and CKD-mineral/bone and anemia care refer to contemporaneous CKD guidance. The 2009 HCV interferon row conflicts with current direct-acting-antiviral practice and is retained only as a labeled historical source value, not as a current default. Drug labeling, viral resistance, vaccine availability, and pregnancy safety have also changed since 2009.
