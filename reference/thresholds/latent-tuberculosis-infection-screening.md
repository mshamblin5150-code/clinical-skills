## Conflicts

No unresolved same-population, same-quantity conflict was identified. The USPSTF
recommendation covers asymptomatic adults age 18 years or older at increased risk and
does not cover symptomatic adults or children and adolescents. Populations whose
screening is disease management, premedication care, public health surveillance, or
employee surveillance were not reviewed and are not treated as exceptions to the
recommendation.

The source reports separate TST positivity thresholds of `5 mm`, `10 mm`, and `15 mm`
with different sensitivity and specificity estimates. These are test-performance
quantities rather than competing universal thresholds, and the source does not select
one threshold for every screened adult. The one-time and annual frequency values apply
to distinct future-exposure populations and remain evidence-absent reasonable
approaches, not an evidence-established universal interval.

The CDC/ATS/IDSA, AAP/ACOG, and AAFP actions are retained as attributed external
guidance with their own treatment-benefit, early-pregnancy high-risk, and increased-risk
populations. They are not rewritten as USPSTF recommendations. Current CDC treatment
guidance is referenced by the source; this sheet does not infer a full drug regimen from
that reference.
