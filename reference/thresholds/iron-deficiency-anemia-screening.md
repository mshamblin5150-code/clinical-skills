## Conflicts

No unresolved same-population, same-quantity conflict was identified. The USPSTF makes
no recommendation for or against screening asymptomatic United States children ages
6-24 months and establishes no screening test or interval. The external CDC, Institute
of Medicine, and American Academy of Pediatrics schedules are retained as externally
reported actions with their distinct high-risk, feeding, prematurity, anemia-result, or
universal populations; they are not imported as USPSTF exceptions. The external AAFP
position agrees with the USPSTF evidence conclusion.

Prematurity and low birth weight appear both as risk factors and as exclusions from the
USPSTF recommendation population. This is not normalized into a screening action: the
risk statement describes association, while the I statement does not apply to those
children. Likewise, hemoglobin or hematocrit is often the first step, but the USPSTF says
the evidence is insufficient to recommend a specific screening test.

The dietary allowances and vegetarian requirement are explicitly attributed to the
Institute of Medicine and retained as prevention context. They do not reverse the
USPSTF's decision to issue no physician-prescribed supplementation recommendation in
this update.
