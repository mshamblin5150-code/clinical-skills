## Conflicts

No same-population, same-quantity conflict was identified. The USPSTF I statement is
neither a recommendation for nor against screening, so the external annual AASM action
for specified high-risk adults is not rewritten as a USPSTF conflict. The external VA
actions apply to patients who report sleep issues or have listed comorbidities, and the
external ACP sleep-study action applies to unexplained daytime sleepiness; those
populations differ from asymptomatic adults and adults whose symptoms are unrecognized.

The source defines obstructive sleep apnea as **more than 5** events per hour while its
separate mild-severity band begins at **5** events per hour. Both source-stated boundaries
are preserved under distinct diagnostic-definition and severity quantities; the sheet
does not silently normalize the exactly-5 boundary. The source establishes no USPSTF
screening interval; the only retained interval action is the explicitly external annual
AASM guidance.
