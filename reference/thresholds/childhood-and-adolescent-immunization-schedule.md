## Conflicts

**CONFLICT: hpv-age-schedule** - The source says children aged `9-10 years` can receive
HPV vaccine and children aged `11-12 years` should receive it; the recommendation
strength represented by the schedule key differs by age group.

**CONFLICT: menacwy-age-schedule** - The source schedules MenACWY at `11-12 years` and
again at `16 years`; these are the two age points represented for the 2-dose healthy
adolescent series.

**CONFLICT: influenza-dose-count** - The source states `1 dose each year` for children
generally and `2 doses` for some children aged 6 months through 8 years; prior
vaccination history determines which count applies.

**CONFLICT: meningococcal-dose-count** - The general table states `2 doses` with
additional doses potentially needed, while the healthy-adolescent footnote separately
states `MenACWY 2 doses` and `MenB 2 doses if needed`; vaccine type and medical
condition explain the distinct values.

**CONFLICT: pertussis-dose-count** - The source states `1 dose at age 11-12 years`,
`additional doses if missed childhood doses`, and `1 dose every pregnancy`; age,
catch-up status, and pregnancy define the different actions.

**CONFLICT: tetanus-dose-count** - The source states `1 dose at age 11-12 years`,
`additional doses if missed childhood doses`, and `1 dose for dirty wounds`; age,
catch-up status, and wound indication define the different actions.
