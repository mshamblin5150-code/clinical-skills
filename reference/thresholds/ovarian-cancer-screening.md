## Conflicts

No same-population, same-quantity conflict was identified. USPSTF, ACOG, the American
Cancer Society, American College of Radiology, and American Academy of Family Physicians
are concordant against population screening in average-risk or asymptomatic women.

The ACOG statement that evaluation of high-risk women may include TVUS, CA-125, and
physical examination applies to a distinct hereditary/high-risk pathway that the USPSTF
screening recommendation excludes. It is not imported as an exception for average-risk
screening. Likewise, TVUS, CA-125, pelvic examination, repeat testing, and surgery used
to evaluate symptoms or abnormal results are not screening instructions.

No USPSTF screening interval exists because routine screening by any method is not
recommended. Annual or every-6-month schedules described in trials are evidence designs,
not guidance, and are not promoted to patient actions.
