**Scoped out under ADR 0009's decision-point rule:** prevalence figures, trial counts
and sample sizes, confidence intervals, heterogeneity statistics, publication dates,
author information, public-comment dates, and study-only demographics or outcome

## Conflicts

No unresolved same-population, same-quantity machine conflict was identified. The p4
related USPSTF table is represented by nine combined BMI-plus-metabolic-risk
populations. It individualizes counseling at normal weight without abnormal glucose,
and at overweight without listed metabolic risk; it provides or refers to intensive
counseling for obesity, for overweight with hypertension or dyslipidemia, and at every
displayed BMI when abnormal glucose or diabetes is present. These are source-era 2018
related pathways, not replacements for this sheet's exact Grade B BMI >=30 action.

The source describes a 5% or greater goal, typical duration and contact patterns, and
named delivery formats as evidence-supported program characteristics rather than one
mandatory regimen. Pharmacotherapy findings come from selectively enrolled trials in
which both groups received behavioral counseling; they are not a USPSTF medication
recommendation or a current drug-selection list. Surgery and nonsurgical devices are
outside the reviewed primary-care scope. Related and external screening/counseling rows
retain their organizations' provenance and are explicitly dated to the 2018 source.
