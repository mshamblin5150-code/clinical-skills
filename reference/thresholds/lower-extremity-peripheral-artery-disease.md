## Conflicts

No same-source, same-population contradictory values were identified. Different ABI, TBI, toe-pressure, TcPO2, and SPP thresholds represent different methods or clinical purposes rather than conflicts. In particular, toe pressure <30 mm Hg identifies severe ischemia, whereas TcPO2 >30 mm Hg predicts wound healing; they are distinct perfusion quantities in method-specific populations. The guideline's every-visit foot inspection recommendation and the externally derived IWGDF risk-stratified 1- to 12-month screening intervals are complementary: the former is a brief inspection at any encounter, whereas the latter describes comprehensive risk-based screening cadence. The FDA simvastatin 80-mg warning and IWGDF foot-screening cadence are labeled external provenance rather than AHA/ACC-origin values.
