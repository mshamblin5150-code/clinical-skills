## Conflicts

CONFLICT: debridement-abscess-size uses >2 cm for the formal recommendation, >=2 cm for prior suggested drainage practice, and <1 cm for limited medical-resolution observations.

The formal conditional recommendation uses **>2 cm** for debridement in a clinically
stable child. Its evidence narrative separately says drainage of abscesses **>=2 cm**
has been suggested and reports medical resolution in small numbers with abscesses
**<1 cm**. These are not interchangeable cutoffs: the first is the panel's recommendation,
the second is prior suggested practice, and the third is a limited observation rather
than a recommendation.
