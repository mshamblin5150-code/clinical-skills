## Conflicts

CONFLICT: bmi-category-definition | pediatric-bmi-classification | `overweight: BMI between the 85th and 95th percentile for age and sex` versus `high BMI: BMI >=95th percentile for age and sex`; the source's traditional category wording includes the 95th percentile in the first range while separately defining high BMI at or above that same percentile, so the printed boundary is preserved rather than silently normalized.

No other same-population, same-quantity conflict was identified. The USPSTF recommendation and externally reported guidance are labeled separately and use distinct action quantities; pharmacotherapy uncertainty supports the USPSTF choice to prioritize behavioral intervention rather than constituting an alternative drug recommendation.
