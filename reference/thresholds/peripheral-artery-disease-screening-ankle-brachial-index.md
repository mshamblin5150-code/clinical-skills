## Conflicts

CONFLICT: abi-abnormal-threshold | adult-abi-measurement | `a ratio <1 is considered abnormal; <0.9 is the typical clinical definition of PAD` versus `the source also labels ABI <=0.9 as low and indicating PAD` versus `the cited Danish screening study defined PAD as ABI <0.9 or >1.4`; the strict and inclusive 0.9 boundaries and the study-specific high-ABI limb are preserved rather than normalized.

The USPSTF establishes no positive high-risk screening action or interval. External
ACC/AHA guidance screens all adults at age 65 or older and younger adults with stated
risk combinations, while Society for Vascular Surgery guidance uses age older than 70
or other listed high-risk findings and recommends against screening without PAD risk,
history, signs, or symptoms. These differing external thresholds remain attributed and
are not imported into the USPSTF I statement.

The source's symptomatic-PAD treatment actions and related USPSTF CVD-prevention topics
are not evidence that ABI screening benefits asymptomatic adults. ABI-directed aspirin
and exercise trials in asymptomatic low-ABI/PAD populations found no significant benefit.
