## Conflicts

No same-population, same-quantity conflict was identified. The USPSTF I statement is
neither for nor against screening and establishes no screening interval. The external
AAO and AOA baseline evaluation at age 40 and the AAO age-specific examination
intervals therefore remain separately attributed external guidance rather than being
rewritten as USPSTF actions or conflicts. For higher-risk persons, AAO bases timing and
frequency on risk but supplies no specific schedule; none is inferred here.

Tonometry alone is inadequate because increased IOP is neither necessary nor sufficient
for diagnosis. This does not conflict with including tonometry as one component of a
combined diagnostic evaluation. The source's risk associations do not create an
exception to the I statement because no tool reliably identifies who would benefit and
evidence for screening higher-risk Black and Hispanic/Latino adults is inadequate.
