## Conflicts

No same-population, same-quantity conflicting patient-action values were identified.
The differing counseling intensities are distinct intervention examples rather than
competing requirements, and the four gestational-weight ranges apply to different
prepregnancy BMI populations.

ADR 0009 disposition:

- the USPSTF Grade B offer recommendation, the separate identify/offer-or-refer
  implementation action, timing and contact ranges, singleton gestational-weight
  targets, BMI definitions, and visually transcribed Table 2 examples were retained;
- Table 2's low <=2, moderate 3-11, and high >=12 contact categories and each
  intervention's nonnumeric mode/content definition were retained. These values
  describe reviewed effective interventions and are not rewritten as mandatory
  prescriptions; the separate >=12-contact effectiveness finding remains explicitly
  comparative evidence rather than a required minimum;
- trial enrollment ages, sample sizes, follow-up periods, risk ratios, confidence
  intervals, heterogeneity statistics, absolute risk differences, mean differences,
  prevalence estimates, and effect sizes were treated as evidence rather than new
  patient-action thresholds;
- the research example of maternal age older than 34 years was scoped out as a research
  need, not a current counseling threshold;
- the related BMI >=30 adult-obesity action was retained because the statement itself
  presents it as another USPSTF clinician recommendation; external resource URLs and
  recommendations without a numeric action were not converted into additional rows;
- article dates, publication metadata, author affiliations, disclosures, citation
  numbers, journal volumes/pages, and reference-list values were not interpreted as
  patient actions.
