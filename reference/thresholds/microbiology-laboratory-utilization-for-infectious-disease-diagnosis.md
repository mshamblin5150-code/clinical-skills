## Harms, uncertainty, and implementation boundaries

- Poor specimens, delayed transport, incorrect temperature, insufficient volume, antibiotics before collection, nonvalidated assays, and contamination can cause false-negative, false-positive, or clinically misleading results. A negative result never overrides a strongly incompatible specimen or timing history.
- Multiplex molecular panels detect only listed targets and can detect colonization, shedding, latent virus, or multiple organisms of uncertain relevance. Culture remains necessary when susceptibility testing, isolate submission, outbreak work, or off-panel organisms matter.
- Serology can reflect remote infection, vaccination, cross-reactivity, passive antibody, or delayed response. Paired acute/convalescent specimens, antibody indices, confirmatory testing, or NAAT are required where the table or narrative specifies them.
- Culture of high-consequence, select-agent, dimorphic-fungal, mycobacterial, rabies, mpox, measles, or other public-health pathogens requires advance laboratory/public-health notification, biosafety controls, and legally compliant packaging. The source-printed CDC and manufacturer directions are external operational authorities, not IDSA/ASM-origin thresholds.
- Do not delay emergency treatment or infection-control action for microbiologic confirmation in suspected bacterial meningitis, encephalitis, necrotizing infection, severe malaria, rabies exposure, measles, mpox, or other time-critical syndromes.
- Pediatric, pregnancy, transplant, immunocompromised, and device-associated branches are not interchangeable with routine adult testing. Volumes and matrices must be adjusted without silently applying adult collection targets.

## Conflicts

Apparent differences in transport time, temperature, specimen volume, assay, repeat interval, or positivity threshold resolve by specimen type, transport medium, organism, illness phase, host, collection method, or diagnostic purpose. External CDC, FDA/manufacturer, ACOG, and public-health instructions printed in the source are labeled external implementation authorities rather than IDSA/ASM-authored values.
