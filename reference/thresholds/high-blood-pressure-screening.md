## Conflicts

CONFLICT: age-13-bp-definition | exactly-age-13 | `child definition: 3 visits at or above the 95th percentile or above 130/80 mm Hg, whichever is lower` versus `adolescent definition: stage 1 is 130/80-139/89 mm Hg and stage 2 is above 140/90 mm Hg`; the statement describes the child rule as applying through age 13 and the adolescent rule as beginning at age 13, so the printed boundary overlaps at exactly age 13 and is preserved rather than silently normalized.

No internal same-population screening-interval conflict was identified. The USPSTF states that no good evidence establishes how often to measure pediatric blood pressure; page 5 separately reports the AAP annual/every-high-risk-visit schedule and AHA/NHLBI routine age-3 schedule. Those positions remain explicitly `EXTERNAL` and are not merged into the USPSTF I statement or treated as evidence that settles its interval uncertainty.
