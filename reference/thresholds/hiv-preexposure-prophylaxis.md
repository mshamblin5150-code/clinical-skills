## Conflicts

No same-population, same-quantity internal USPSTF conflict was identified. Separate
method-dependent quantities preserve TDF/FTC and injectable cabotegravir for sexually
acquired risk at weight 35 kg or greater and TAF/FTC for the complementary population
that excludes receptive-vaginal-sex risk. The source
also preserves the regulatory/guidance distinction for injection-drug-use risk: no
PrEP medication has that FDA-approved indication, while externally reported CDC
guidance says persons who inject drugs are likely to benefit from any FDA-approved
PrEP medication. The external page-8 positions are qualified and are not merged into
the USPSTF recommendation.
