## Conflicts

The USPSTF adolescent I statement does not recommend for or against screening and
directs clinicians to use judgment. Source-printed AAP guidance instead screens
adolescents through their early 20s annually and at specified visits or triggers, while
Bright Futures screens all adolescents, including ages 18 to 21 years, within a
psychosocial history. These are organization-specific positions with overlapping but
nonidentical ages, settings, substances, and evidence judgments; they are not rows with
one shared quantity and population.

The source-printed ACOG schedules also remain distinct: annual screening concerns
nonmedical prescription-drug use in women; age 18 to 26 years concerns preventive care;
universal screening applies before and early in pregnancy; postpartum screening is as
indicated. The USPSTF adult recommendation covers all adults age 18 years or older,
including pregnant and postpartum persons, but establishes no optimal repeat interval.
These branches are not collapsed into a false schedule conflict.
