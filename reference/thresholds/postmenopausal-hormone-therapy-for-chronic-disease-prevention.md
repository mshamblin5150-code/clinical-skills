## Conflicts

No same-population, same-quantity conflict was identified. Combined estrogen and
progestin and estrogen alone have different uterus-status populations and different
benefit and harm profiles, but both have no net benefit for primary chronic-disease
prevention and both carry Grade D recommendations.

FDA-approved symptom treatment and external ACOG/NAMS symptom positions have a distinct
treatment purpose from primary chronic-disease prevention. Osteoporosis prevention is
itself preventive, so its FDA indication and the external ACOG/NAMS osteoporosis
positions require a different reconciliation: the source acknowledges reduced fractures,
but concludes that the overall benefits and harms produce no net benefit at the population
level. The regulatory indication and external positions therefore do not negate the
USPSTF Grade D population-level chronic-disease-prevention recommendations.
