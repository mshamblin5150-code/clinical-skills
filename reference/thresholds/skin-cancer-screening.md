## Conflicts

No same-population, same-quantity conflict was identified. The I statement is neither a
recommendation for nor against screening. It therefore is not rewritten as agreement
with the external observation that no US professional organization currently recommends
clinical visual screening.

The American Academy of Dermatology has no formal clinician-screening guideline but
encourages free public screening events. Those are distinct provenance claims, not a
formal recommendation and event-level recommendation conflict. Related USPSTF counseling
actions concern UV-exposure behavior or self-examination counseling, not clinician visual
screening, and remain separate.

The source establishes no USPSTF screening interval. The every-2-year German national
program is retained only as a study-design boundary and is not promoted to guidance. It
also establishes no referral cutoff or destination: a symptomatic suspicious lesion is
outside screening, and biopsy is the stated requirement for definitive diagnosis.
