# Cardiac Arrest and Life-Threatening Toxicity Due to Poisoning

<!-- schema: threshold-sheet/2 -->

This sheet records decision points only; it is not a substitute for the focused update. The guideline cautions that ideal antidote doses are often unknown and directs clinicians to a medical or clinical toxicologist or regional poison center.
