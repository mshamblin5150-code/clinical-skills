## Conflicts

CONFLICT: external-population-screening-position | external-general-population | `EXTERNAL (American Society for Clinical Pathology): recommends against population-based screening` versus `EXTERNAL (American Academy of Family Physicians): supports the 2014 USPSTF insufficient-evidence recommendation for general-population screening`; the ASCP advises against population screening, whereas the AAFP adopts the 2014 USPSTF insufficient-evidence position, which is not a recommendation for or against screening.

The USPSTF I statement applies to its asymptomatic, community-dwelling, nonpregnant
adult population. The Endocrine Society position against population screening for
individuals not at risk and the Endocrine Society and American Association of Clinical
Endocrinologists risk-based screening positions remain attributed external guidance
with differently stated populations; they are not rewritten as USPSTF conclusions.

The National Academy of Medicine context that needs are met at `20 ng/mL (49.9
nmol/L)` for an estimated 97.5% of the population, that bone-health deficiency risk
begins below `12 to 20 ng/mL (29.9-49.9 nmol/L)`, and treatment-trial definitions from
`<20 ng/mL to <31.2 ng/mL` are different quantities and evidentiary purposes. The
source explicitly states that no one serum cutoff defines deficiency and no consensus
defines optimal health or sufficiency; none of these values is promoted to a universal
screening or treatment threshold.
