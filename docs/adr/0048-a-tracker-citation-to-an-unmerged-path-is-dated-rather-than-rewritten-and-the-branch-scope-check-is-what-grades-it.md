# A tracker citation to an unmerged path is dated rather than rewritten, and the branch-scope check is what grades it

[#516](https://github.com/mshamblin5150-code/clinical-skills/issues/516) was filed by [#466](https://github.com/mshamblin5150-code/clinical-skills/issues/466)'s sweep on 2026-08-25 over fourteen open tickets carrying a `blob/main/docs/adr/` link to a record that was not on `main`. Its sharpest sentence is that the sweep that found it had committed the same defect minutes earlier. Seven further sessions then recorded themselves as instances — #456, #483, #496, #497, #498, #502 and #511 — every one of them with `adr_next.py`'s own output on screen, which had just told it the number was claimed in a tree not on `main`.

Grilled on 2026-08-27, against `origin/main` `fb2892f`. The clinician ruled every point below on the same day. **Nothing is built here; this is the record the build reads.**

## What is ruled

1. **The defect is the undated assertion, not the dead link.** A `blob/main` citation to an unmerged path is *correct-and-early*: it 404s for a window and then heals itself when the record lands. The four sweeps that treated the 404 as the defect chose link hygiene — cite by branch or PR, rewrite to `blob/main` on merge — and each produced the opposite defect, a *not yet on `main`* qualifier that goes stale silently on merge and is reachable by no walk. [#516](https://github.com/mshamblin5150-code/clinical-skills/issues/516)'s 2026-08-26T00:53:32Z comment tabulates the asymmetry end to end after 19 such qualifiers went stale in one merge. Ruling 1 dissolves both: a **dated** citation is true before the merge and still true after it, so nothing is ever rewritten and the link starts working by itself.
2. **The record is graded, not the link.** The qualifier is a record-level line, so no proximity window exists to define. That window is where a link-keyed predicate goes wrong, and this thread has already watched two sweep matchers fire on live line-anchored links and on this ticket's own fenced illustrative URL.
3. **Both a written convention and a command that grades it**, on `tracker_bodies.py`'s precedent in this same subsystem. That module's docstring records the alternative failing here already: *"Decision 2 of that ticket was answered in prose four days before it was asked … and the bodies kept being lost anyway."* This is [#214](https://github.com/mshamblin5150-code/clinical-skills/issues/214)'s *what a written instruction cannot do is fail*, and the resolution is not check-instead-of-prose but prose **and** a grader.
4. **It fires on the publication event, not on a harvest.** A third step in [`.github/workflows/tracker.yml`](../../.github/workflows/tracker.yml), which already fires on the five tracker events and already checks out the default branch, so `git ls-tree` costs nothing and no `scratch/` harvest is involved. The workflow's own header argues it: *"Re-harvesting the whole tracker on every comment would replay #264's ruled historical findings forever."* A harvest-wide check is permanently red on day one, and a permanently red advisory check is one people stop reading — which is `spelling_scan`'s recorded `licence` failure.
5. **A finding is a red advisory run plus a step summary**, `tracker_scan.py`'s posture in the step beside it. It does **not** post a comment. The merge-receipt precedent is one immutable receipt per merge, a low-frequency high-value event; this is a per-comment lint that fired seven times in two days, and the bot's own comment would retrigger the workflow on `issue_comment: created`. The honest ceiling is stated rather than glossed: the workflow guarantees **the detector ran**, not that anybody read it.
6. **This widens `tracker_branch_scope.py` rather than adding a module.** Its trigger becomes `in_flight or self_declares_completion or cites_an_unresolved_path`. The widening also revives a dormant check: **zero of the 46 open issues carry `in flight`**, so the one class of session guaranteed to cite an unmerged record — a grilling that has just run `adr_next.py` — was invisible to it.
7. **Two forms satisfy the trigger.** The existing `> **Branch state:** …` line at `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md:106` stays canonical and nothing already written stops working. A second accepted form names the cited path — `> **Cited record state:** \`<path>\` is not on \`main\` as of \`YYYY-MM-DD\`.` — because the existing line hardcodes `is not on \`main\`` **about the record's own branch**, so a record with no unmerged branch of its own that cites somebody else's open PR would have to assert a falsehood to pass a check it correctly triggered. The `Branch state:` line remains a proxy for the cited path's location; that imprecision is accepted and declared, the unavailability is not.
8. **The mention-versus-use rule is adopted from `tracker_bodies.py` rather than reinvented** — `FENCED_CODE` and `CODE_SPAN` stripped before the residue is read. Without it the check fires on #516, on every sweep comment on that thread, and on this record. That is `differential_scan.py`'s [#153](https://github.com/mshamblin5150-code/clinical-skills/issues/153) — *describing the rule broke the tool that checks the rule* — for the fifth recorded time in this repo. The escape hatch it opens is accepted, on the same terms `spelling_scan`, `differential_scan` and `tracker_bodies` already accept it.
9. **The surface is the whole of what `tracker.yml` fires on** — issue bodies, issue comments, pull-request bodies and pull-request review comments. `grade()`'s `"pull_request" in issue` early return is right for the `in flight` trigger and says nothing about whether a PR body may cite an unmerged path undated, so the exemption is kept for the old trigger and not applied to the new one, stated in the module rather than left as a shared early return. **The ADR PR body is the decisive case**: every ADR reaches `main` through a pull request whose body describes a record that is by definition not yet on `main`.
10. **A repo-relative Markdown link destination in tracker text is a second, narrow row.** `[ADR 0036](docs/adr/0036-….md)` in an issue comment resolves against the *issue* URL and 404s unconditionally — no merge fixes it. It is the only form on this thread that is not correct-and-early, and it shares the ruling 8 strip.
11. **The bare-number citation is not graded and the convention names it instead.** A bare `ADR 0042` asserts nothing about where the record is, so under ruling 1 it cannot be undated-and-wrong. A predicate for *an ADR number with no link near it* would fire on #516, on every sweep comment on that thread, on `adr_next.py`'s output and on this record. **The Goodhart residue is declared rather than dismissed**: once the check exists, the cheapest way to pass it is to stop linking, and the convention is the only thing pushing back.
12. **What is not graded is declared in one module object**, on `reference_scan.NOT_REACHED`'s arrangement and [ADR 0046](0046-the-scope-summary-is-graded-in-one-direction-and-the-unread-list-is-the-span-table.md) ruling 9's terms — [`docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`](../agents/issue-tracker.md) points at it and copies no row, and a test binds both directions. Whether the **existing** trigger's implicit limits migrate into it is that module's own population question, filed rather than settled here, which is ADR 0046 ruling 9's own scoping reused.
13. **The module keeps its name.** Both triggers ask one question — *does this text date its relationship to `main`?* — so `branch_scope` is not going stale, and `CLAUDE.md`'s tool-arrival ledger sentence *"`tracker_merge_receipt.py` and `tracker_branch_scope.py` were the two before it — written on one #290 branch on the same terms"* is dated history a rename would falsify. What changes is the report: each finding names **which trigger fired**, since `grade()` already returns a `reason` string and currently distinguishes only two cases.
14. **Nothing is repaired.** Zero `blob/main` dead links of this class are live; every instance the thread recorded healed by merge, exactly as its 2026-08-26T13:41:43Z comment predicted and measured. The nine surviving qualifier records stay: editing them makes each comment disagree with the state it was posted in, which is #497's own stated reason for leaving its ruling comment as written, and [#212](https://github.com/mshamblin5150-code/clinical-skills/issues/212) found that GitHub retains a pre-edit revision of every edited record with no API to delete one.
15. **The citation-coordinate class is a separate ticket, not a widening.** #510 cites *"ADR 0030 ruling 9"*; that record carries exactly eight numbered rulings. It is not ruling 1's defect — never early, never self-healing, and dating it changes nothing — and ruling 4's placement structurally cannot reach it, because `git ls-tree` returns **paths** and a coordinate needs **contents**. It is the mirror of [#538](https://github.com/mshamblin5150-code/clinical-skills/issues/538)'s ADR 0016 case, and both are tree-content checks while this is the merge-state axis.

## The measurement came first, and it is the first one taken with the ruled predicate

Re-derived 2026-08-27 at `origin/main` `fb2892f`, over 46 open issues and every issue comment, with the ruling 8 strip and with `#fragment` and `?query` removed before resolution. The harvest was deleted immediately after.

| | |
| --- | ---: |
| records read | 2,203 |
| `blob\|tree\|raw /main/` occurrences in prose | 277 |
| dead paths | **1**, across 1 record |
| records carrying a *not (yet) on `main`* phrase | 9 |

**Every prior count on that thread used a matcher without the ruling 8 strip**, and two of them are on record producing false positives against #516's own fenced text. This one did not fire on it.

**The one dead path is not this class.** It is `docs/adr/0016-…-a-ratified-record-s-facts-…md` — a misspelled slug for a record that has been on `main` throughout, owned by [#538](https://github.com/mshamblin5150-code/clinical-skills/issues/538). This is the first evidence that the two classes are **disjoint** rather than an argument that they are.

**The figures are this record's to state and nothing committed re-derives them.** They are counted against a harvest of the tracker's entire text, which `tracker_scan.py` classes as something that lands in `scratch/`, and the next comment anybody writes moves them. The build's test is what makes the *predicate* re-derivable; the population is not.

**The denominator is a floor on one further axis, declared rather than left to be found.** Ruling 9 puts pull-request bodies and review comments in scope, and this measurement excluded them — it is issues-only, because it inherited `grade()`'s current gate. #496's sweep recorded one of its four instances landing in a pull-request description, so the excluded surface is known to be non-empty.

## Why the convention half cannot carry this alone, stated once

Seven sessions wrote the defect in two days. The tell is uniform and it is not carelessness: **a correct link and a dead one are byte-identical until the branch merges**, so no author reviewing their own text can see it — #497's formulation, and the reason the ordinary sequence produces it unprompted. Every one of those sessions had, in its own terminal, `adr_next.py`'s report that it had enumerated between 11 and 15 worktrees precisely to avoid numbers that are *not on `main`*. #498's ran 11. The information needed to write the correct line was on screen every single time.

**Ruling 1 makes the convention as cheap as it can ever be** — one act at write time, appended once, never revisited — and that is a materially better proposition than the two-step *rewrite on merge* every sweep had been rejecting. It is still not a mechanism, which is why ruling 3 keeps both halves.

## What this record does not claim

**Not that instances will be caught at write time.** Ruling 4's event fires *after* publication, seconds later. The author is usually still in-session and #498 measured that repair at four records and about two minutes, but the check is a backstop, not an interception.

**Not that the count stays at zero.** It was zero at `88c888e` on 2026-08-26 and false again within hours, with nothing in between having failed. It is zero again today. The class is governed after this, not extinguished.

*Ruling 7's second form is written out here rather than only in `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md` because it does not exist yet; the sheet is where it becomes canonical.*
