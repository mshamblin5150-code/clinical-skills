## Ownership, guards and bounded evidence

`parallel_reader_scheduler.py` is reused unchanged. The new adapter binds each reserved ordinal to its exact owner, raw capture identities, clone/lane identity, interpreter, payload, source/manifest, and distinct TEMP leaf before launch. Partial native starts remain attempted rows. Neither a global subprocess monkeypatch nor the most-recent Session owner is used for concurrent attribution.

Every live stream retains its unchanged 16 MiB cap and 1 GiB actual free-space floor. Both live owners are checked; a second-stream failure cancels/drains both trees. Sequential cleanup waits are bounded by min(5 seconds, remaining global hard time), so the first failed drain cannot consume all remaining cleanup time. Process-owner handles, access, sharing and lifecycle policy are unchanged. The separate compact-only file capability is specified below. Private captures stay local and are never uploaded.

Source, refs, raw-object inventories, environment, corpus, eight-field Windows metadata with ChangeTime, reparse/hardlink/length/identity checks, capture screening, and generated-output admissions are reused without widening. Completion is not assumed to be a prefix. Missing and unread sets are explicit. Cancelled and incomplete children never become completed through cleanup recovery. Summed wall/owned-wall/tree-CPU/analysis metrics count each attempted child once; absent metrics remain null. First finite failure context is preserved before later publication/cleanup failures.

Pin-set mismatches are qualification refusals after the attempted ledger has been constructed, not reasons to discard that ledger. The receipt retains exact hashed unpinned/stale sets and remains unqualified. An earlier completed scheduler snapshot is retained separately if later owner cleanup or identity evidence degrades; such a pair is unread for final qualification, never successful by inference from its earlier snapshot. Cleanup failure is captured before receipt/publication failures can replace it.

The exact public route shares the existing aggregate 4 MiB output budget, including reused helper events and a terminal reserve. Hard-deadline crossing before or during terminal publication cannot return success. No partial receipt can authorize the next arm. ## Compact retention is an explicit evidence tradeoff

This route is compact-only. It does not fall back to the rejected retained-log packet. Historical captures, existing receipts and previously frozen evidence are never candidates for disposal. Every new capture pair is reserved before creation and authenticated against its exact registered owner, capture root, native file identity, coherent eight-field metadata, length and hash.

Generic `Session.run` never disposes. The observed-session rereads, original parser and direct semantic consumer must all finish first. Git materialization is not a final consumer: source HEAD/status/ref/raw-inventory and clone predicates retain their raw commands through their explicit enclosing guard checkpoint. Wrong HEAD/ref/status and interrupted scopes retain their captures. Dirty first status remains the primary refusal even when its optional diagnostic commands are denied. Nested scopes count each pending command once. Expected detached-head return code one is eligible only after its original exact predicate, not from exit code alone.

The planted method retains its complete 22-pair evidence through its authentic assertions and trailing guards. Each measured pair is retired only after coherent raw parsing, original classification, reference equality, owner cleanup and metrics. Its lane cannot launch the next lookup until retirement completes. Expected reader-marked nonzero refusal is classified success; unexpected execution failures and unread or cleanup-unverified results remain raw. Unexpected observations bypass success-only compact validation and are returned unchanged to the original scheduler. Retained failure pairs continue occupying the same two-pair measured window, while only live streams reserve additional growth. Earlier already-dispatched work may still finish its next lookup after its prior successful pair is retired; no new module is admitted after the original error signal. Canonical first-error resolution is unchanged. The first execution failure is retained. Earlier successful raw output may already have been disposed and cannot be restored for a later unrelated arm/source failure.

For eligible new pairs only, the compact adapter opens each exact capture with `GENERIC_READ | DELETE`, `OPEN_EXISTING`, `FILE_SHARE_READ`, no-follow/sequential flags. It reauthenticates registered identity and exact parsed bytes through the retained handle. Existing read-only helpers and owner APIs remain unchanged. There is no ACL change, share widening, delete-on-close flag, path-unlink fallback or destructive retry. A fresh work-deadline check precedes each destructive mark; an already-started mark still receives exactly-once handle cleanup, but cleanup time cannot authorize a second mark. Generated-output admissions are not widened.

Before either disposition mark, an exclusive new receipt-only x+b writer is checked readable before its metadata query, then written, flushed, fsynced and closed. The writer-to-reader handoff preserves volume/file identity, creation time, link count, type/reparse attributes, length and exact bytes while allowing only the receipt writer’s own LastWriteTime/ChangeTime to finalize on close. The full coherent metadata baseline is established after close through the acquired read-only handle. That same authenticated read-only/no-write/no-delete sharing handle is retained through both raw dispositions, outcome receipt verification and final authority recheck. The raw-capture eight-field predicate is unchanged. Outcome and terminal receipts are separate finite files. The compact store permits at most 2,184 pairs, three records per pair and 16 KiB per record: at most 6,552 records and 107,347,968 logical content bytes. The public summary includes exact reserved/retired/pending counts and a digest of the ordered receipt triplets, without raw text or private paths.

Flush-acknowledged readback is not a universal power-loss or directory-durability guarantee. Two-file disposition is not atomic: if the second operation fails, the first may already be marked or closed. Remaining evidence is retained and partial or unknown disposal makes the comparison inconclusive. A crash may close a marked handle before a terminal record exists. Missing, corrupt or uncertain outcome records mean unknown raw retention, never proof that both originals remain; no destructive retry is allowed. A terminal file cannot attest its own final successful readback/close, so overall success additionally requires the containing invocation's verified completion. Marked and closed is not proof of secure erasure, pathname absence or reclaimed space.

The 18 inner native-control pairs and four redirected private streams are retained. Successful outer-control compact receipts preserve the entire validated parsed result, and original `files_retained=True` still describes those inner captures. No new native qualification command or inner capture pair is added in this implementation. Real Windows compact-capability qualification is a separate held proposal, not inferred from portable injected tests.

## Compact-lifecycle capacity admission, not a quota or runner-size assumption

Per-stream caps remain 16 MiB. A new maximum of 32 simultaneously pending helper pairs includes all enclosing/nested source guards and planted pairs. The slot is counted before launch and is released only after verified disposition. The 1,600 total outer-command ceiling is an explicit packet limit, not a historical observer guarantee. Global or pending exhaustion refuses without deleting unqualified evidence. A source-clone checkpoint has 11 + R pending calls and a nested guard peak of 8, so the 32-pair ceiling requires at most 13 deleted refs per clone. Initial preflight peaks at 12 pending calls; planted work peaks at 22 + 8 = 30. These necessary eligibility conditions appear in the admission receipt. High-ref clones may exceed the tighter pending checkpoint limit and must refuse; ref population is not assumed.

The initial raw allowance is 32 pending helper pairs (1,024 MiB), two measured pairs (64 MiB), 18 retained inner pairs (576 MiB), and four retained redirected streams (64 MiB), totaling 1,728 MiB. Each measured retirement completes before that lane's next launch. Completed but undisposed helper bytes already reduce actual free space; they consume pending slots but no remaining growth allowance. Live streams reserve only remaining capacity, and unoccupied pending slots reserve their full potential capacity. Parent and nested scopes never add the same pair twice.

Evidence-volume admission adds a 1 GiB floor, 4 GiB TEMP planning headroom, 128 MiB auxiliary planning allowance and the separate 102.375 MiB compact-content maximum. The initial nominal evidence reserve is 7,422,214,144 bytes, or 6.9124755859375 GiB. The three original arm/final receipts are individually capped at 4 MiB within the auxiliary allowance. Logical receipt sizes are not physical allocation bounds across thousands of files; metadata/allocation overhead and unknown TEMP growth can consume the planning headroom. No deleted-byte amount is credited. Only fresh observed free space controls admission.

Completed inner-control slots leave the future-growth reserve only after validated completion; retained bytes remain reflected in actual free space. The existing Session live-poll callback checks the authenticated evidence and clone volumes. Refusal cancels only its registered owner through the unchanged native method, preserves the primary cause if cancellation fails, and leaves cleanup visible. Both measured owners are checked by their original live guards.

The clone-volume reserve remains a 1 GiB floor, twice the largest measured complete seed/lane logical inventory times remaining clone count, and 4 GiB generated-growth planning headroom. Full `.git` history is included, and any enumeration error refuses rather than undercounting. Four future clones initially add `5 GiB + 8 × logical basis` on a distinct volume; a shared volume uses one floor, for `10.9124755859375 GiB + 8 × logical basis` initially. Clone multiplication, TEMP/generated headroom and auxiliary allowance are estimates, not physical storage quotas or proven peak-growth bounds. Inadequate actual free capacity is an inconclusive refusal, not a reason to lower capture caps or switch retention policies.

The owned-command call graph remains 1,294 plus the sum of deleted refs across four clones on success, with at most three dirty-first-status diagnostic calls on refusal. The 1,600 global ceiling leaves 303 ref-dependent slots after that explicit failure allowance, but the new 32-pair checkpoint ceiling can refuse earlier. The necessary per-clone R <= 13 condition limits a successful call graph to 1,346 commands; it is not a guarantee that time, capacity or other qualification succeeds. All commands, including Git and planted work, are counted.
